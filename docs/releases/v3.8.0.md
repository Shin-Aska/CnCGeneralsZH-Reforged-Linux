**Water that follows the map, volumetric clouds beyond the old eight-field limit, and upstream 3.8.0.**

Zero Hour Reforged Linux 3.8.0 brings this fork up to upstream 3.8.0 and includes
our Linux improvements since 3.5.1. Runs natively on 64-bit Linux with SDL3 and
Vulkan.

### Flow-aware Modern water

- **Waves follow the coastline.** Swell bends through channels, slows and turns
  in shallow water, and wraps around islands. Sheltered branches and enclosed
  lakes stay calmer; independent coastal seas receive their own waves.
- **More natural surface detail.** Fine wind ripples keep a prevailing heading
  over open sea while channel detail follows its local route. Separate routes
  retain their movement where they meet behind islands.
- **Less repeated rendering work.** Water reuses terrain, shoreline, geometry
  and reflection calculations, including while panning and zooming. Static
  preparation moves into map loading, and open sea uses a smaller shader.
- **Choose the movement you prefer.** **Options → Water → Water Movement Behavior**
  keeps the new map-aware movement when on, or restores 3.5.1 movement when off.
  This choice applies on the next map load. Existing water quality controls remain.

Legacy, SSR, Hybrid and experimental Path traced water reflections remain
available. Hybrid is the initial reflection mode when Modern water is selected;
Path traced reflections still depend on the scene, GPU and chosen detail level.

### Volumetric clouds without the old field cap

Linux now draws recognized toxin, anthrax and radiation fields through one shared
volume. The previous eight-layer admission limit no longer sends additional
fields back to Classic puddles. Local checks covered 128 simultaneous fields,
including overlapping fields and all four palettes.

This removes a fixed field-count limit, not GPU or memory limits. More sources
still cost work, and a larger visible area can reduce local detail. Rendering
work is spread across frames; unsupported effects or unavailable rendering
resources retain their Classic appearance.

- **Contaminate works while firing.** The Toxin Tractor's green, Anthrax Beta and
  Gamma ground effects are volumetric during spraying and after it stops.
- **Normal toxin impacts match.** Tractor attacks, Toxin Rebels and Toxin Tunnels
  use volumetric ground splashes, including the brief Gamma splash. Repeated
  impacts at the same spot share a cloud instead of multiplying its opacity.
- **Attached puddles follow their source.** Tractor puddles and damaged GLA toxin
  tank leaks use their world position and actual particle lifetime. Stopping or
  destroying an emitter lets its remaining effect expire. Decorative tank leaks
  gain no damage or gameplay hazard.

These cloud changes preserve damage, weapon timing and game-state checksums.

### Also included from upstream 3.6.0–3.8.0

- **EA's four veterancy levels return:** Regular, Veteran, Elite and Heroic.
  The expanded rank ladder, its extra perks and leadership auras are removed.
  Supply loads return to EA's $75 for workers, $300 for trucks and $600 for Chinooks.
- **Mod support is removed.** The previous mod-loading workflow is no longer
  available in this version.
- Smarter skirmish AI uses its general's signature units, avoids known anti-air
  with aircraft, captures resources earlier and can establish additional bases.
  Its income bonus now responds to how its team is doing.
- Ground vehicles turn faster and carry movement through bends. Target selection,
  stream weapons, aircraft behavior and mission cutscene timing receive fixes.
- Updated faction command bars, picture-based production queues, observer tools,
  tooltips, hotkey controls, additional player colors and per-seat handicaps.
- Further work on experimental Turkiye, including its own buildings and unit art,
  defenses and radar-guided weapons; random-map generation also receives fixes.

Gameplay changes can make older replays play out differently. Generated-map names
from 3.5.1 or earlier are rejected: their replays cannot play, and lobbies or saved
games referring to them cannot rebuild those maps.

### Enable the enhanced graphics

New profiles start with **Classic graphics, Classic water and Classic clouds**.
Explicit saved choices are retained; a profile without a saved Classic graphics
choice now starts in Classic.

To use the enhancements, turn off **Classic graphics** under **Options → Graphics**,
accept and restart, then select **Modern water** and **Volumetric clouds**.

### Downloads

Choose the **AppImage**, **portable `.tar.zst`**, **DEB**, **RPM**, **Arch package**
or **Flatpak bundle** below. These are engine-only Linux x86_64 packages built
through GitHub Actions from this origin's `v3.8.0` tag.

**Bring your own legally owned Generals and Zero Hour installation.** Original
game data and optional upscaled Reforged art are not included. Native packages
target glibc 2.31 or newer with a working Vulkan driver; CI's minimum Ubuntu target
is 22.04. The Flatpak uses its declared Freedesktop runtime.

`SHA256SUMS` covers the six game packages. Download all six listed files for a
complete `sha256sum --check SHA256SUMS` check. The launch PNG is a separate media
asset.

CI checks builds, tests and package installation/startup across Debian, Ubuntu,
Fedora, openSUSE and Arch, using Mesa Lavapipe/Xvfb for renderer checks.
Asset-dependent tests may skip; these checks do not establish gameplay or hardware
compatibility on every distribution.
