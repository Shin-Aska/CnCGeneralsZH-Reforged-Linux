### Modern water

The Linux renderer now skips a repeated CPU calculation for open-water vertices when the wave surface
is already available. This reduces the work needed to draw large stretches of unobstructed water.

### Cold Fusion Reactor control rods

With HD models enabled, the control rods now light up as the upgrade extends and stay lit when
you zoom out. At distances where the reactor model omits the glow, the game draws the nearest
detail level that includes it. Reactors awaiting the upgrade stay dark.

### Downloads

Choose the AppImage, portable archive, DEB, RPM, Arch package, or Flatpak bundle. The packages use
your existing Generals and Zero Hour files. Multiplayer requires matching game versions and bundled
data. You can verify the downloads with `sha256sum --check SHA256SUMS`.
