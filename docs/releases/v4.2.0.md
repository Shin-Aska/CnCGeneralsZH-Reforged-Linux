**Upstream 4.2.0 with optional HD packs you can download from inside the Linux game.**

### Optional HD assets

Open **Options → Graphics → Manage HD Assets** to download HD textures and terrain, detailed unit models, detailed map models, and online skins. Pick individual packs or use **Download Full Set (10.63 GB)**. On first launch, you can download the texture pack (1.30 GB), choose packs, or skip.

![The in-game Manage HD Assets panel on the Graphics tab, showing four packs with individual Download buttons, Download Sources, and Download Full Set](https://github.com/Shin-Aska/CnCGeneralsZH-Reforged-Linux/releases/download/v4.2.0/v4.2.0-launch.png)

Use **Download Sources** to add, edit, or remove custom HTTPS mirrors. The official source is tried first, and every pack is checked against its upstream SHA-256 hash.

You can remove packs installed through the manager. Downloads and removals take effect after a restart. To use the enhanced art, turn off **Classic Graphics** on the Graphics tab, then enable **HD Textures** and **Detailed Models** on the Display tab.

- Adds Brutal, Vicious, Savage, and Insane AI difficulties.

This Linux release also carries forward the fork’s graphics and campaign improvements. [Upstream 4.2.0 release notes](https://github.com/olcayseygan/CnCGeneralsZH-Reforged/releases/tag/v4.2.0)

### Downloads

Choose the **AppImage**, **portable archive**, **DEB**, **RPM**, **Arch package**, or **Flatpak bundle**. Verify the six packages with `sha256sum --check SHA256SUMS`.

The packages include the Linux engine and fork overlay. You’ll need your own Generals and Zero Hour installation and a working Vulkan driver. Native packages target x86_64 Linux with glibc 2.31 or newer. Flatpak uses its declared runtime.
