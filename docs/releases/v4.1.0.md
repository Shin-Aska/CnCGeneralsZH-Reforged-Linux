**Upstream 4.1.0 with the Linux graphics improvements from 4.0.1.**

Zero Hour Reforged Linux 4.1.0 runs natively on 64-bit Linux with SDL3 and Vulkan.

### Upstream 4.1.0

- Redesigned online screens with Play vs AI, generated-map improvements, and AI difficulty driven by a rating.
- An updated Red Alert sidebar, revised explosions, and the Splinter skin.
- This fork keeps **Hotkey Scheme** and **HUD Layout** as separate choices.

### Linux graphics

- The reworked FFT Modern water from 4.0.1, with map-aware waves, filtered sun glints, and toxic-water material controls.
- Native lighting, soft and GPU particles, weather, heat distortion, ambient occlusion, and internal HDR from 4.0.1.
- Native support for upstream's per-pixel terrain sunlight, terrain subdivision, Ground Foliage, and orange bloom with local contrast.

Use the release that recorded your older replays to play them back.

### Downloads

Choose the **AppImage**, **portable `.tar.zst`**, **DEB**, **RPM**, **Arch package**, or **Flatpak bundle**.
`SHA256SUMS` covers all six packages. Download every listed file for a complete `sha256sum --check SHA256SUMS` check.

**Your own legally owned Generals and Zero Hour installation is required.** These packages contain the Linux engine and fork assets. Original game data and optional upscaled art are separate. Native packages target glibc 2.31 or newer with a working Vulkan driver. Flatpak uses its declared runtime.
