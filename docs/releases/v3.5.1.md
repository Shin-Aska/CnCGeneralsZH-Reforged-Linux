**More natural water, better performance, and sharper water reflections.**

Zero Hour Reforged Linux 3.5.1 brings this fork up to upstream 3.5.1 and includes
the Linux graphics improvements made since our 3.4.2 release. Runs natively on
64-bit Linux with SDL3 and Vulkan.

### Modern water

- **More natural movement.** Ocean waves turn toward nearby shores and follow a
  consistent prevailing heading in open water. Foam and boats follow the waves.
- **Better water performance.** Reuses unchanged water geometry and terrain data,
  removes duplicate work, and skips CPU wave calculations already handled by the
  GPU. Animated waves and moving boats continue to update.
- **More room for path tracing on midrange GPUs.** Reduced water overhead makes
  experimental path-traced water reflections more practical. Frame rates depend
  on the map, camera, GPU and chosen detail level.

### Water quality controls

The Water options page lets you balance appearance and performance:

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Scene reflections | On / Off |
| Refraction | Full / Simple / Off |
| Wave detail | High: 256×256 FFT / Low: 128×128 FFT |
| Reflection detail | High / Low |
| Reflection mode | Legacy / SSR / Hybrid / Path traced (experimental) |

The Performance / Quality slider and Low, Medium, High and Ultra presets now set
the water detail controls too. Low disables scene reflections and refraction and
uses Low wave and reflection detail. Your water style and reflection mode remain
as chosen. Click **Accept** to apply and save changes.

### Sharper path-traced water reflections

- Reflected objects retain texture detail, making shapes and surfaces easier to
  recognize than in 3.4.2.
- High reflection detail uses **512×512 captures**, including during camera
  movement. Low uses **256×256 when settled / 128×128 while moving**.
- Better filtering reduces subpixel shimmer; consistent sky handling avoids a
  change in appearance near capture edges.
- Fixes missing reflected geometry reproduced on Intel UHD 770.

Path tracing applies to **water reflections** and remains experimental. High
detail costs more to trace, and moving scenes can still look soft, noisy or
flicker. Hybrid remains the default reflection mode.

### Also included from upstream 3.5.0–3.5.1

- Rank perks and leader auras for USA, China and GLA, with visible aura rings and
  corrected training grades. Hackers no longer give leadership auras or rings.
- Retail-style ground movement with Reforged's visual motion, plus smoother
  rendering on displays faster than 60 Hz.
- Better performance in large air battles, game checksums, collision and sight
  work, fog updates and interface processing.
- Fixes for scripted skirmish AI, release-build map launching, network address
  reporting, aircraft banking and jet wakes.

### Downloads

Choose the **AppImage**, **portable `.tar.zst`**, **DEB**, **RPM**, **Arch package**
or **Flatpak bundle** below. These are engine-only Linux x86_64 packages built
through GitHub Actions from this origin's `v3.5.1` tag.

**Bring your own legally owned Generals and Zero Hour installation.** Original
game data and optional upscaled Reforged art are not included. The native packages
target glibc 2.31 or newer with a working Vulkan driver; CI's minimum Ubuntu target
is 22.04. The Flatpak uses its declared Freedesktop runtime.

`SHA256SUMS` covers the six game packages. Download all six listed files for a
complete `sha256sum --check SHA256SUMS` check. The launch PNG is a separate media
asset.

CI builds and tests with Mesa Lavapipe/Xvfb and checks package installation and
startup across Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, openSUSE and Arch. Asset-dependent tests
may skip; hardware gameplay, full replay CRC parity, Flatpak portals and Steam
Deck gameplay require separate local validation.
