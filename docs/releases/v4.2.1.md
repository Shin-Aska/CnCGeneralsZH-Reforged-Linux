### Cold Fusion Reactor glow

Fixed a longstanding visual issue inherited from retail Zero Hour. At wide zoom, the Super Weapon
General's Cold Fusion Reactor could show the blue upgrade glow before Advanced Control Rods was
purchased. The glow now appears when the rods start extending. The fix applies to retail and HD
models.

Multiplayer requires matching game versions and bundled data.

### Downloads

Choose the AppImage, portable archive, DEB, RPM, Arch package, or Flatpak bundle. Verify the downloads
with `sha256sum --check SHA256SUMS`. The packages use your existing Generals and Zero Hour game files.
