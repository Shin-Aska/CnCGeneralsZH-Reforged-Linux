# Zero Hour Reforged Linux 4.0.1

**Reworked FFT water, more graphical effects on Linux, and clearer, smoother campaign openings.**

4.0.1 is a Linux graphics and campaign update to this fork of
[Zero Hour Reforged / zerohour.gg](https://zerohour.gg). It runs natively on 64-bit Linux
with SDL3 and Vulkan. This update develops the Linux renderer further; it does not
announce a new upstream version synchronization.

This fork tests experimental graphics before selected changes are offered upstream.
Some effects are still being tuned for Zero Hour's renderer and large battles, so
frame rate and memory use can vary with your GPU, map and settings. Classic graphics
and individual effect controls remain available.

## Reworked FFT water

The wave pipeline behind the original Modern water has been reworked with a shared
FFT ocean, a revised wave spectrum, depth-aware wave attenuation, filtered sunlight
glints and wave-driven foam. It retains this fork's map-aware movement through
channels, around islands and along exposed or sheltered shores.

- **Separate movement and filtering controls.** Shore wave turning and Wave filtering
  can be adjusted independently. Wave detail now offers Low (64), Medium (128) and
  High (256), while existing saved Low and High choices retain their previous detail.
- **One map-aware movement path.** The obsolete Water Movement Behavior switch has
  been removed. Old saved Off values no longer restore the earlier movement model.
- **Calmer toxic water.** Matching polluted-water materials now use a
  murky green look, with stronger turbidity, softer reflections and reduced wind-wave
  motion. Bubbles remain enabled as the visual indicator.
- **More material control.** Turbidity, surface roughness, ripple damping and wind
  resistance can be edited independently in the native water preview and exported
  to material rules. Wind resistance reduces actual wind-wave height and chop;
  bubbles and vessel-generated waves remain independent. Surface scum is removed.
- **The preview uses the current game water.** It now prepares the production FFT
  water's depth and direction fields, so material edits are shown on the current path.
- **Water fixes.** Stretched wave streaks near the carrier's stern and sharply turning
  shores are corrected. A missing-shoreline-data guard fixes a Linux startup crash in
  this fork's Modern-water path, including the first original Generals USA mission.

## More effects through native SDL3/Vulkan

Effects previously restricted to upstream's Windows Direct3D 11 renderer now have
native Linux implementations:

- Soft particles, procedural fire, GPU particles, heat haze and blast distortion.
- Per-pixel lighting, Firelight, sky/ground lighting, ambient occlusion and internal
  HDR tone mapping.
- Model status effects, team glow, defence-range overlays and Battle Marks.
- Classic-water foam and impact splashes, atmosphere, and building-collapse effects.

**Weather receives additional work:** rain spells with clear intervals and soft
transitions, drifting snow, blowing sand, gusts, matching ambience and haze/sky
lighting. Maps can begin clear or rainy. Maps with authored rain or snow retain
their own precipitation behavior. Low blowing dust stays off water, and added rain
can leave brief ground splashes and water rings.

**Water and flooded areas receive further polish:** Warm Light haze blends over the
visible Modern-water surface. Submerged flames fade and clip at the waterline,
combustion smoke stops emitting underwater, and an observed fire crossing into water
can produce a brief steam puff. Submerged tree vertices stop swaying while exposed
branches keep their motion.

EMP Pulse and EMP Patriot domes also reach their intended size, and EMP flashes no
longer form screen-wide horizontal bands.

## Campaign voices, timing and presentation

- **Original Generals briefing narration returns.** Configured spoken loading
  briefings start again and can finish even when their movie ends first.
- **Loading dialogue is sequenced.** Challenge taunts play one line at a time while
  map assets continue loading. The transition waits for both, preventing General
  Thrax's loading taunt from talking over his opening introduction.
- **Briefing waits follow the actual cue.** Pre-querying a line no longer starts its
  completion deadline too early. Frozen-world countdowns also give spoken lines the
  intended time instead of cutting them short or allowing unintended overlap.
- **Preloaded speech starts promptly.** Resident dialogue can begin without waiting
  behind unrelated audio loads.
- **Turbo respects cinematic speed.** Single-player cinematics use their authored
  speed, with double-speed gameplay resuming when control returns. This also addresses
  skipped or truncated dialogue in General Thrax's opening.
- **Mission openings render more smoothly.** Scripted camera turns, zooms and tilts
  are smoothed between their original steps. Normal single-player animation timing
  also corrects the repeated backwards jumps in the close-up Raptor flyover.

The automatic presentation fixes target normal single-player play. Replay, network
and capture modes retain their documented rendering-clock exclusions. The separate
live model-smoothing preview remains opt-in and needs further display testing.

## Retained from earlier releases

Underwater clarity, Legacy/SSR/Hybrid and experimental Path traced water reflections,
volumetric radiation/toxin clouds including the extended Contaminate and attached-puddle
effects, Linux sun/smoke shadows, and Asset Preloading remain available. These are not
all new in 4.0.1. The reworked FFT path and newer Linux effects are separate from the
foundations already merged upstream.

[Feature-by-feature upstream status](https://github.com/Shin-Aska/CnCGeneralsZH-Reforged-Linux/blob/v4.0.1/docs/upstream-status.md)

## Enable the enhancements

New profiles start with **Classic graphics, Classic water and Classic clouds**.
Turn off **Options → Graphics → Classic graphics**, accept and restart, then choose
**Modern** water and **Volumetric** clouds. The Effects and Water pages provide the
remaining controls. Existing saved preferences are preserved.

Hybrid remains the initial Modern-water reflection mode. Path traced reflections
are an optional experiment with approximate scene coverage and a higher rendering
cost. Rain-contact rings follow the authored water surface rather than each moving
FFT crest or trough.

## Downloads and validation

The release includes the **AppImage**, **portable `.tar.zst`**, **DEB**,
**RPM**, **Arch package** and **Flatpak bundle**, with `SHA256SUMS` covering the six
game packages. The launch PNG is a separate media asset.

**Your own legally owned Generals and Zero Hour installation is required.** Original
game data and optional upscaled art are not included. Native packages target Linux
x86_64 with glibc 2.31 or newer and a working Vulkan driver; Flatpak uses its declared
runtime.

Public CI covers asset-independent tests with Mesa Lavapipe/Xvfb, six-package
assembly and checksums, and package installation/startup checks. Hardware gameplay,
full campaign/replay coverage, Flatpak portals and Steam Deck gameplay require
separate local validation. Cross-version replay and multiplayer compatibility are
not established by these package checks.
