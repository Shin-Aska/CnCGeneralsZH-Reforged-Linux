# Weather presentation and qualification

This report records the measured **v4** weather implementation. The later [randomized rain-startup follow-up](../weather-rain-startup-2026-11-15/README.md) has its own build and checks; the 27 runs here remain measurements of v4. Its exact schedule header is frozen under `evidence/source-snapshot`, and original raw evidence is unchanged.

This local change expands **Options → Effects → Weather** with gusts, layered drifting snow, low blowing dust, rain surface contacts, matching ambience and atmospheric lighting. Weather remains off for new profiles and suppressed by Classic graphics or a missing presentation provider. It changes client presentation, not unit sight, damage, pathfinding or the simulation random stream.

Enhanced rain arrives in irregular spells instead of falling continuously. The deterministic cosmetic schedule uses 210-second slots, 48–56 seconds of rain including 6–8-second fades, and roughly 138–178-second dry breaks between spells. In measured v4, the first spell starts immediately; later starts vary within their slot. The follow-up randomizes the opening phase using map and match seeds. A map's authored precipitation keeps its existing behavior and takes precedence over these added rain spells. Snow and sand retain continuous weather with variable gusts and density.

Low sand cards follow dry ground. Their complete footprint is checked against the copied rendered water geometry, with a margin at shorelines; unknown cells remain empty until the bounded cache resolves them, then fade in. Higher airborne sand remains sparse. Weather ground haze is applied before water, so actual water rendering covers haze on the bed instead of receiving a blanket dust tint. This does not change the separate Heat Haze option.

Rain contacts follow sampled falling trajectories. Land receives brief spray; water receives expanding rings on the selected rendered polygon or river plane, including its slope. Work is bounded independently of the falling drops. Contact height follows the authored rest surface rather than the instantaneous FFT wave displacement. Modern water therefore needs explicit visual qualification; the arithmetic alone does not establish that rings remain readable over its waves.

Gust, density and the rain envelope drive smoke drift, tree displacement, ambience gain and haze. Trees retain their sway phase, types and random speed factors while displacement and sway period follow the weather continuously. Weather uses existing licensed rain and wind samples through the normal ambient/master mix, with owned handles and separate level-specific event names. An already playing authored emitter or map ambience bed using the source event takes precedence, including one that starts after the added loop. Off, hidden weather, reset, map transition and a missing or muted audio device release the owned loop. Pausing prevents new/retried admission while preserving an established paused loop for resume.

Atmospheric lighting changes haze, horizon and sky tint using the map's own light channels. It does not change the sun, shadows, time of day or world lighting state. A cold night retains its light ratios. During rain fades, the sky blends back to its ordinary map colours. With Warm Light enabled, weather haze runs before water with the storm envelope and ordinary warm haze runs at its existing later phase with the complementary weight. Only one haze contributes at either endpoint. Both contributions reuse the existing haze shader; the transition's extra pass is part of the measured cost.

## Reproduction and measurement

[`reproduction/run_weather.py`](reproduction/run_weather.py) prepares the pond fixture, exact profile and launch command. It accepts an explicit executable, packaged overlay and the reader's own retail root. Existing runtime switches provide the deterministic W3D capture clock, native offscreen rendering, screenshots and timing. An inert screenshot request beyond the quit limit arms that clock without capturing during timing. `--audio` enables the real null-backend audio mixer; it does not provide physical listening evidence. The completed comparison uses ordinary observer ownership with `--idle-ai` (one starting cash and ten-minute peace); fixed AI thinking remains part of the measurement. Zero cash is not used because the existing launcher ignores a zero override. The earlier takeover diagnostic changed human-seat fog/view ownership and is rejected for primary timing. Release also ignores the debug-only `-noshroud` switch, which is omitted from final commands. Qualification pictures, debugger probes and audio captures are separate from timing.

The primary measurement is the existing native **end-to-end offscreen present interval**, in milliseconds. It is neither a hardware GPU timestamp nor an FPS claim. All compared runs use the same camera, seed, fixture, resolution, runtime assets and options, apart from the explicit Weather on/off variant. MapLight is on in the phase benchmark so the complementary haze pass is exercised. Other enhancements are held fixed in the recorded profiles.

Rain is reported by active plateau, fade-in, fade-out and fully dry phases, together with a complete schedule slot and its occupancy. The active comparison includes a full 30 simulated seconds of gust variation. A full-cycle average does not replace active-storm cost. [`reproduction/summarize_weather.py`](reproduction/summarize_weather.py) obtains the exact phase boundaries from the compiled production `test_weather_particle_math --rain-window` helper and a hashed production-header phase-series helper using the qualified current-draw capture-clock formula. Baseline continuous rain and final Weather off are labelled by the matching enhanced schedule to compare identical absolute scene times. Every repeat, sample count, median, p95, maximum and outlier is retained; no arbitrary performance pass threshold is used.

## Recorded validation

- Release builds of the integrated renderer, weather arithmetic and audio/native atmosphere tests pass.
- Weather arithmetic covers long-running finite motion, the old one-hour boundary, invalid/paused steps, 15/20/30/60 FPS contact admission, sloped river normals, dry-mask geometry and rain-spell boundaries.
- The native Vulkan atmosphere test passes 5,405 checks, including actual pixels, pre-water weather versus later warm haze, complementary endpoint/half weights, duplicate rejection and strict scene/depth/map identity checks.
- Focused CPU CTest passes 10/10, including environment producer/postprocess/shader checks; existing weather classification/wrapping tests pass 2 tests/11 checks and extreme snow INI rejection passes 1 test/29 checks.
- The owned audio gain regression passes 1 test/15 checks, including two same-event handles, category mute, captured paused silence, resume and removal. Actual final-mix captures and scene lifecycle qualification also passed within the [observed matrix](qualification.md); physical speaker listening remains unobserved.

All 27 primary comparisons passed admission, retaining 253,800 raw interval rows. Active weather has a measured cost increase: about +0.3 ms median, +0.4 ms mean and +0.9–1.0 ms p95 on this scene. Rain fades cost more; fully dry rain is near the off control. The [performance report](performance.md) gives all phase costs, repeat ranges and complete-slot occupancy without masking active cost. The [qualification matrix](qualification.md) links exact-final day/night/options images, visible flat-pond rings, shoreline sand motion, natural wet/dry/return, audio, lifecycle and HDR16F results, with unobservable/unrun boundaries explicit. The [portable reproduction guide](reproduction/README.md) covers raw-data verification and new private runs.

The earlier dark takeover pictures are rejected as brightness/performance qualification: the actual view was a non-observer human seat with shroud about 125, versus the ordinary observer's fully visible 255. Afternoon lights were identical. This was a harness ownership difference, not night lighting or a weather exposure regression.
